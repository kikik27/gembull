# GemBull brand guide

**Name:** GemBull. **Testnet token symbol:** GEMBULL at `gembull-core.testnet`. **Tagline:** “The meme runs on rituals.” The older RITL Testnet contract is a separate historical deployment. Name, domain, social handle, and trademark clearance remain open.

## Visual identity

- Background `#070A08`, surface `#101611`, neon lime `#B7FF16`, off-white `#F4F7F2`, success green `#00B85A`. Reserve gold `#FFC857` for reward or achievement details.
- Use the official vector master emblem `/brand/gembull-symbol.svg` and primary lockup `/brand/gembull-logo.svg` for scalable UI, headers, and print. Use `/brand/icons/gembull-app-icon.svg` or `/brand/icons/gembull-app-icon.png` for compact marks, favicon, and app icon. The full asset package is organized in [`docs/brand/pack/`](pack/PACK_README.md).
- The approved mascot identity is a dark bull with horns, green sunglasses, and a black hoodie. Transparent GM, checked-in, streak, and target-hit stickers preserve that character. Do not use the N-like mark from the earlier concept board as the project logo.
- Keep headings short, high contrast, and readable. Use type and spacing before adding glow or animation.

## Claims and disclosure

- The ritual is the primary product. Show actual chain values with their network/source, and label proposed values as preview.
- The stock reserve and any future points/allocation records are **SIMULATED** concepts until separate integrations are implemented and tested. No visual may imply real shares, verified custody, redemption, or dividends. The revised MVP has no stock receipt token.
- Do not promise returns, APY, price targets, guaranteed rewards, or launch timing.
- Do not present the generated campaign coin as the deployed token icon or as an investment product.

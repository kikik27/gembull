### Post 3 — Missed target

### Post 4 — Rules on-chain
The goal: genesis parameters that are publicly readable and cannot be tuned by an admin after launch. We will publish the contract address and reproducible tests when the testnet build is ready.\n\n#BuildInPublic #NEARHackathon

### Post 5 — Stock reserve layer
Important distinction: RITL is not a share. The stock-reserve and receipt layer in our hackathon MVP is simulated. A real equity-backed product would require a valid issuer, custodian, legal rights and verified acquisition.\n\n#RWA #Transparency #NEAR

### Post 6 — Sybil limitations
A wallet is not a human. Holding thresholds and one check-in per account reduce simple farming, but cannot prove unique personhood. We disclose the limits instead of pretending otherwise.\n\n#Web3 #OnchainIdentity #NEAR
